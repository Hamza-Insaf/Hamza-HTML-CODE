Rainbow

A rainbow appears when sunlight enters a raindrop, bends (refraction), reflects off the back of the drop, and bends again as it exits. Each wavelength of light bends by a slightly different amount, which spreads the colors out into a spectrum. The classic order — red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet — is due to red light bending the least and violet bending the most.
