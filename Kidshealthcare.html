💪 Why Your Body Needs Exercise

Your body needs exercise to stay healthy and strong. Regular physical activity helps improve cardiovascular health, build muscle and bone strength, enhance flexibility, and boost mental well-being. Engaging in activities like running, swimming, cycling, or playing sports can also help maintain a healthy weight and reduce the risk of chronic diseases such as diabetes and heart disease.

⚫ Quick Health Tips:

  • 🍎 Eat 5 fruits and vegetables daily
  • 💧 Drink 8 glasses of water daily
  • 🛌 Get 7-9 hours of sleep each night
  • 🧘‍♂️ Practice mindfulness and relaxation
